How to play

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a real word of the right length.

After each guess the tiles change colour:

PLANT

P is in the word and in the right spot.

STONE

T is in the word but in a different spot.

CRANE

N is not in the word.

Coins

Solve a word to earn coins. The fewer guesses you need, the more you earn (coins by number of guesses):

Spend coins on a hint, which greys out 2 letters that aren't in the word, or on 6 more tries (once per word).

Change the word length with the menu at the top. When a round ends you'll see what the word means.

Out of guesses

Buy 6 more tries to keep going, or see the answer.
